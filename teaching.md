## Teaching Activities


I teach <b>Languages of parallelism</b> for the HPC and Data Science master’s degree organized by the University of Bordeaux and Bordeaux INP since 2019.

I have previously taught classes on Introduction to research at the University of Bordeaux, Tree Structures, and Algorithm at the Enseirb-Matmeca engineering school, but also Parallel Algorithms and Programming (Univ. of Grenoble Alpes), Initiation to C programming (INP Grenoble) when I was a postdoc at Inria, Advanced compilation: addition of a profiling pass in GCC, Parallel optimization techniques, and C programming and UNIX Environment at the University of Versailles when I was a Ph.D student. 

I have recently taught <b>Convergence calcul haute performance et intelligence artificielle</b>, an Inria Academy course (<a href="https://www.inria-academy.fr/formation/convergence-hpc-et-ia/" target="_blank">link</a>).
